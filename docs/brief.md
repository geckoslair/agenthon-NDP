# The brief

Source: event portal, "Brief" tab, revealed at 10:28 on 21 November 2026.
Snapshot taken at 10:37. Quoted text is verbatim.

## Track 1 · Agentic AI in companies

Track sponsor: Ariete Tech Solutions.

> Build an AI your team would trust.
>
> Build an AI that works in the background and asks before it acts, then
> defend it live against a jury and rival teams trying to break it.
>
> Flashy demos don't win here. The winner is the build that is right, admits
> what it doesn't know, and never acts without a person's approval.

Two challenges, one track, one jury, one ranking. **We chose Challenge B.**

## Challenge B · Back Office Autopilot (ours)

> The colleague who does, and asks first.
>
> Pick one back-office process and run it end to end. The rules are known,
> but every case has exceptions: your autopilot runs the whole process, stops
> at the exceptions, and asks a person before anything that can't be undone.
>
> Any process: Invoices · Payments · Reminders · Payroll inputs · Expense
> reports

Your solution must:

1. **Run end to end, with a person in the right places.** "Raw input in,
   finished output out. It stops at exceptions and waits for approval before
   anything is sent, paid or exported."
2. **Solve an urgent business problem.** "Show who loses time or money today,
   how much, and what your autopilot saves."
3. **Leave a trail.** "Show the log of what it did, on which input, and who
   approved it."

## The data

> - If you know the industry's real data formats, make your own synthetic
>   data.
> - If you don't, start from an open-source dataset.
>
> No real client or personal data.

## Challenge A · Company Brain (not ours, for context)

A tool that watches a team's work, keeps a shared memory that people curate,
and tells each person what needs attention before they ask. Seven yes/no
checks: speaks first, people curate the memory, knows who is asking, cites,
admits gaps, only proposes, fits the team's day.

Some of these are worth borrowing for B because the jury values them across
the track: **cite the source of every extracted value, admit gaps, only
propose**.

## The pitch

About three minutes, in The Stage, in front of the jury, who can ask
questions. "Show the problem, the solution and the demo: a demo that works
beats ten slides." Only 8 to 12 teams pitch; the jury picks the finalists
from all submitted projects, so the repository and description must stand on
their own.
